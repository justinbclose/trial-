A Reimagined Stay

Quiet Side of the Club

“Torrey Pines meets Spanish-style warmth, with the easy rhythm of the California coast.”

Set back from the sand and wrapped in courtyard greenery, our garden-view Club Rooms trade ocean spray for shade, birdsong and the hush of a walled garden — with the beach, the courts and the water still only a short stroll away.

Ninety years on the same stretch of shoreline — and a garden that has grown up alongside it.

The Room

Designed Around the Light

Every garden-view room is being reworked from the floor plan up: bathrooms and kitchens expanded, sightlines opened toward the courtyard, and windows put to work so daylight carries all the way through the room.

Inside, the palette leans into sand, surf and sky — deep navy against light oak, brass that warms as it ages, and textures drawn straight from the bluff trails above the cove.
